## What 2026 shows

- Extra-polar sea-surface temperature reached 21.1°C on 22 August 2026 (Copernicus C3S). That is an all-time daily record in the season when the ocean normally cools.
- 2025 set another record for upper-2,000 m ocean heat content — the ninth consecutive annual record.
- El Niño is present and strengthening. That is the fast Air driver for the next two seasons.
- NOAA PSL TNA and TSA run through June 2026. Early-2026 CEG is slightly negative. Niño 3.4 does not explain CEG.
